This limited check identifies a threatened piece and requires both a supported protective choice and a losing choice before assessing the played move. It checks up to 16 candidate moves to establish an opportunity, then checks the move you played. Unproved threats, unfamiliar histories, unsupported replies and time limits remain unresolved. A missed choice is a narrow observation, not a complete assessment of the move.

Several opportunities can come from one game. Different game contexts and overlapping playing times limit comparisons. These observations do not establish improvement or cause.

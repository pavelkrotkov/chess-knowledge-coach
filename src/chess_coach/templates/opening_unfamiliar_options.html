How the center-choice check works

This optional learning fit asks whether a branch offers positions to practice an observed early central pawn capture choice. At least three independent current games of the same side and first-turn context must all show the learner choosing the capture over a same-search, equal-depth alternative. Game-start times must be distinct and the owned time control known and consistent in the form positive seconds plus a nonnegative increment (for example, 600+0). Overlapping games do not establish decision order.

At least two public branches need five complete, legal 20-ply games each and five valid four-ply profiles. The four-ply profile may include later occurrences of the same parent and move. The 20-ply availability count uses only the exact first turn or reply. Unknown or short games widen the observed sample range. A recommendation requires one range to exceed every other qualified range by at least 50 percentage points. Overlap, ties, mixed or sparse evidence remain exploratory.

The public archive is a bounded, nonrandom convenience sample. Public availability counts positions with both choices, not the move a public player chose. Its index does not record time control. Counts are not population probabilities, skill, soundness or a general style assessment.

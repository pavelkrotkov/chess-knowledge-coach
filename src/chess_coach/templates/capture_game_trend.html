The proof covers non-checking direct captures of non-pawn pieces with no immediate recapture and at least three material units retained after every supported immediate reply. Checking captures, promotions, unsupported replies, uncertain history and limited proofs remain unresolved. A supported legal reply that leaves less than three material units disproves that narrow capture opportunity. Unsupported replies remain unresolved unless another supported reply disproves it. A different move can have compensating merits; an other move is not necessarily a mistake.

Counts can be sparse, and several opportunities can occur in one game. Game context and overlapping game times limit comparisons. These observations do not establish improvement or cause.

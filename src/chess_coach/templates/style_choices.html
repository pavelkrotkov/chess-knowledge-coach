Comparable means distinct legal roots in the same current complete MultiPV search, exact centipawn scores within 50 cp, the same reported depth and at least depth 4, with four legal plies in each compared line. These are bounded estimates, not proof that choices are equally good. A short comparable alternative or an alternative with an unusable score suppresses the comparison.

At the four-ply endpoints, simplification compares total remaining knights, bishops, rooks and queens and requires a difference of at least two pieces. Material uses pawn-unit values 1/3/3/5/9. The activity proxy counts distinct enemy-half squares attacked by the learner's knights, bishops, rooks and queens, requiring a difference of at least three squares opposite to a material difference of at least one pawn. The attack map includes pinned pieces, occupied or defended squares and attacks during check; it is neither legal mobility nor initiative. Future PVs are projections, not what necessarily happened.

Queenless choices contrast zero queens with one queen per side at the endpoints, starting with one per side. Imbalanced queen endpoints are ambiguous. Low-material transitions contrast fewer than six total non-pawn pieces against six or more, starting with at least six. This is a phase proxy, not proof of an endgame. Original standard-start histories are replayed; repetitions, terminal or claimable draw positions, custom starts and promotions in the compared horizon are excluded. The projected lines are conditional on both players’ future moves, not observed transitions or intent.

A qualifying move is a non-checking direct capture of a non-pawn piece worth at least three material units, with no immediate recapture and at least three units still ahead after every supported immediate reply. A choice can be resolved even when another capture remains unproved. A supported reply leaving less than three units refutes this narrow opportunity; unsupported replies remain unresolved unless another supported reply refutes it. The proof does not assess later tactics or compensation; a different move can have other merits. Checking captures, promotions, special positions and replies the proof cannot resolve remain unknown.

The audit checks at most 1,000 recorded positions, a 1 MB game record, 32 capture candidates per turn, at most 64 immediate replies per candidate and a shared {{ report.time_limit_seconds }}-second proof deadline. Source replay and rechecking are separately bounded by the game limits. Individual chess operations cannot be interrupted mid-operation. Only recorded moves can be checked; omitted moves cannot be detected.

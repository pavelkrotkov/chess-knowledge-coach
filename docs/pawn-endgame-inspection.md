# Private pawn-endgame inspection

`chess-coach inspect-pawn POSITION_ID` and the private decision page inspect a
current learner-owned decision. The source is rechecked after the search; a
changed source or supplied stale fingerprint never returns the old proof. No
facts, mappings, reviews, attempts or mastery are written. No position is sent
to a remote service. Automatic coaching and training remain in #135.

## Scope and result

Standard legal boards with the two kings and one or two pawns, at most one
pawn per side, are supported. Castling and all other compositions are outside
scope. Legal en passant, both colors, pawn captures, double pushes and all four
promotion types are considered. A promotion is not itself a win. Checkmate,
stalemate, insufficient material, fivefold repetition and the 75-move rule are
handled through the rules library, with checkmate taking precedence. Either
player may choose an available threefold or fifty-move claim; the values assume
optimal play and optimal claims. A claim is not automatic. There is no inference
about intent, skill, mastery, or the outcome from opposition geometry.

The local WDL value is used only at a zero halfmove clock: immediately after a
pawn move or capture, or at a known reset starting state. Such a move makes all
earlier board repetitions irrelevant. Otherwise legal moves are searched until
a reset or terminal result, with lower and upper bounds from the current mover's
perspective. An exact result is returned only when those bounds coincide.
Cursed wins and blessed losses (native WDL +1 and -1) are retained in the
`wdl:` reason and treated as draws under the stated optimal-claim policy. Each
alternative's bounds are inverted to the original mover's perspective; its
`wdl:` reason is the native **child side to move** value. A known result does
not require a unique move. Unknown alternatives must not be treated as wrong.
The inspection lists counts of native WDL categories encountered in the root and alternative search; those values use the probed side-to-move perspective. The one-move SAN/uci alternatives are legal witnesses to their displayed
bounds, not a complete winning line or strategy. No DTZ or mate distance is
reported.

The search shares a maximum of 6,000 nodes and five plies across the root and
all alternatives, including terminal/probe nodes. Earlier alternatives may use
the budget before later ones; unknown is not a loss. History is reconstructed
from the complete, verified stored PGN, capped by the existing 1,000-decision
source limit. For a custom-FEN starting position with a nonzero halfmove clock,
prior repetition history is unknown: proof remains unknown until a recorded
pawn move or capture resets the clock. A custom root with clock zero is treated
as a known reset; this relies on the FEN clock being truthful. A standalone FEN
with unknown preceding history is not promoted into a full game history.

## Reference data and verification

The 20 files in `src/chess_coach/data/pawn_wdl` are deliberately vendored public
reference data, not learner content. They total 951,104 bytes. The manifest pins
exact filenames, byte sizes, SHA-256 and canonical embedded checksums. A
hard-coded manifest SHA-256 protects its contents. Runtime reads at most this
bounded data set, verifies every file against both SHA-256 and the canonical
embedded checksum, opens only allowlisted files and limits open descriptors to
four. Missing, altered or incomplete resources make the proof unknown. The
SHA-256 pins the exact bytes whose embedded checksums were independently
recomputed by the upstream checker; runtime does not rely on the footer alone.

Source: [Lichess 3–5 man WDL mirror](https://tablebase.lichess.ovh/tables/standard/3-4-5-wdl/),
with its [SHA-256 manifest](https://tablebase.lichess.ovh/tables/standard/sha256)
and [embedded-checksum manifest](https://tablebase.lichess.ovh/tables/standard/tbcheck.txt).
The [generator and redistribution terms](https://github.com/syzygy1/tb/blob/0bb8aeee525f364bb750f96df312a1a7c9b54398/README.md)
are pinned to Ronald de Man's commit `0bb8aeee525f364bb750f96df312a1a7c9b54398`.
The author states files generated by that generator may be freely redistributed;
the source code has separate GPL terms. Each embedded checksum matches the
[generator's canonical list](https://github.com/syzygy1/tb/blob/0bb8aeee525f364bb750f96df312a1a7c9b54398/checksums/wdl345.txt).
The [syzygy-tables.info SHA-256 list](https://github.com/niklasf/syzygy-tables.info/blob/master/checksums/sha256)
corroborates the published hashes, but is not evidence of independent generation.

At acquisition, the pinned generator's `tbcheck` was compiled project-locally
and recomputed every file's checksum, all 20 reporting OK. Each file was read
with a 15-second network timeout, an exact per-file size limit and a total cap
of 1,200,000 bytes, and was written only after verification. No learner data or
positions were transmitted. The WDL closure includes KPvK and KPvKP, all
three-piece promotion results, each promoted piece versus the remaining pawn,
and all two-promoted-piece combinations; captures reduce to those three-piece
results or bare kings. Future compositions require their own verified closure.

[python-chess documents](https://python-chess.readthedocs.io/en/latest/syzygy.html)
the reset assumption, child-side POV, transitive subtable requirements and
WDL categories. It warns that corrupted files have undefined behavior. The
bundled set is verified before any tablebase file is probed.

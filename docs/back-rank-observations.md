# Bounded back-rank observations

The existing bounded mate cursor also processes the independently versioned
back-rank family. A back-rank checkmate requires the defending king on its home
rank and an actual rook or queen checker on that rank. Short mating claims use
legal witnesses against every legal defense, preserving the game history and
draw rules. Proof is limited to three plies, 6,000 nodes and 100 replies.
Unsupported, incomplete and exhausted searches remain unknown.

Defensive comparisons enumerate legal learner moves and every immediate reply
under a shared 6,000-node budget. An alternative is prepared only when no
immediate checkmate of any kind follows. An opponent-turn geometry probe only
nominates moves; a pre-existing positional threat requires the same mating move
on a real legal quiet alternative. Otherwise a played move is described only as
allowing or avoiding a concrete immediate back-rank mate. Legal counterfactual
moves are not recorded as actual game moves. Draw-claim availability and
terminal alternatives are retained. The result does not establish overall
safety, best defense, material compensation, intent or ability.

The private decision and evidence pages retain proof limits, alternatives and
source currentness. Prepared own-game lines revalidate ownership, review state,
current source and witnesses. Reveal is accounted for; unlisted legal moves
need verification. New facts and mappings are provisional and their mastery
gate remains closed pending independent positive, negative and ambiguous
held-out validation. Accepted older mate evidence and reviews are preserved.
